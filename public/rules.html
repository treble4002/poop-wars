📜 Rules & privacy

The rules

  1. 13 and up only. You must be 13 or older to make an account. When you sign up, you confirm this.
  2. Be kind. Trash talk is for silly fun. No slurs, threats, bullying, sexual stuff or sharing personal info. Our filter blocks the worst words, and breaking the rules can get you removed.
  3. Trash talk is wiped after each game. Messages are deleted when the game ends, and anything older than 24 hours is deleted too.
  4. Report abuse. Tap 🚩 on a message to report it. We keep a copy of only that message so we can review it. You can also block a player, and they can't challenge or message you after that.

Privacy

  • We store your silly name, your email (used to sign in), your games and scores, and the time you agreed to these rules.
  • We don't ask for your birthday. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics.
  • Delete your account: contact the Poop Wars team from the email you signed up with and ask us to delete it. We'll remove your account and your data.
